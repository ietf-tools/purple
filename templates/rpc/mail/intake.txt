Template for the intake form: questions sent to the authors once their
document is in the RFC Editor queue. The subject and recipients are set in
rpc.api.RfcMailTemplatesList.

The items under "To be sent as needed" apply only to some documents. The
cluster and errata items are filled in, or left out, from the document's
cluster and the RFCs it obsoletes or updates; the editor removes any of the
others that don't apply before sending.
{% endcomment %}Author(s),

Congratulations, your document has been successfully added to the RFC Editor queue!
The team at the RFC Production Center (RPC) is looking forward to working with you.

To help reduce processing time and improve editing accuracy, please confer with
your coauthors and respond to the questions below.  Only one author needs to reply
to this message.

Notes:

* If you need/want to make updates to your document, we encourage you to make those
changes and resubmit to the Datatracker.

* If you feel no updates to the document are necessary, please reply with any
applicable rationale/comments.

* The RPC team will not work on your document until we receive a
reply.  After we hear from you, your document will start moving through the queue.

Please feel free to contact us with any questions you may have at
rfc-editor@rfc-editor.org.

Thank you!
The RPC Team

--

1) Please review and confirm that the Authors' Addresses, Contributors, and
Acknowledgments sections are current.


2) Please share any style information that could help us with editing your
document. For example:

* Is your document's format or its terminology based on another document,
WG style guide, etc.?

* Is there a general pattern of term capitalization or formatting that the
editors can follow (e.g., "<tt/> should be used for token names.")?


3) Please carefully review the reference entries in the References section
to confirm that their information is current and that any URLs resolve to
the correct source.  Unless otherwise specified, the references will be
updated per standard RFC style, as described at:
https://authors.ietf.org/en/reference-style-guidance.


4) Is there any text that requires special handling? For example,
are there any contentious sections that should be edited more carefully?
